# Professional Photo Guide for Viva Plant Nutrition

## 🎯 Goal

Create a professional, authentic, and reassuring visual identity that conveys:
- **Professional expertise** of the nutritionist
- **Empathy and closeness** with pregnant clients
- **Authenticity** and naturalness
- **Human connection** between nutritionist and client
